About

Beyond Minute Bot is a desktop content automation tool designed to help create, review, and publish educational short-form videos to connected social media accounts.

The project focuses on educational content about science, technology, space, nature, and the future.

Social Media Publishing

Users authorize supported social media accounts before any publishing actions are performed.

Videos are reviewed before publication and are only published after user approval.
