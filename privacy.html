Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. About Beyond Minute Bot

Beyond Minute Bot is a desktop content automation tool that helps users create, review, and publish educational short-form videos to social media accounts authorized by the user.

2. Information We Process

When a user connects a supported social media account, Beyond Minute Bot may process information required by the platform's API, including:

  • Basic account identification information.
  • Authorization tokens provided by the social media platform.
  • Video files selected or generated for publication.
  • Publication status and technical API responses.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used only to provide the application's functionality, including authenticating connected accounts, preparing videos, publishing approved content, and checking publication status.

4. User Authorization

Beyond Minute Bot does not publish content to a social media account without authorization from the account owner.

Users may revoke platform access through the settings provided by the applicable social media service.

5. Data Storage

The current desktop version of Beyond Minute Bot operates primarily on the user's local computer.

Authentication credentials and application data may be stored locally when necessary to maintain authorized API access.

6. Data Sharing

Beyond Minute Bot does not sell personal information.

Information is transmitted to connected social media platforms only when required to perform user-authorized actions through their official APIs.

7. Third-Party Services

Beyond Minute Bot may interact with third-party platforms such as TikTok, YouTube, and other supported social media services. Their own privacy policies and terms apply when using their services.

8. Data Deletion

Users may revoke access to connected social media accounts at any time.

Locally stored application data can also be removed from the user's computer.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's functionality or integrations change.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact the operator of Beyond Minute Bot through the official project repository or support channel.
